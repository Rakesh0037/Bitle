How to play

  
Every day there's a short piece of embedded C. Work out the final 32-bit value of REG and type it as 8 hex digits. You have 6 tries.

  
After each guess the digits change colour:

  
A right digit, right place

     3 digit is in the answer, but somewhere else

     F digit is not in the answer

  
Assumes a 32-bit ARM Cortex-M (32-bit int, little-endian, standard alignment) unless the puzzle says otherwise. Win or lose, you get a short explanation of the idea behind it.
